02. What Are Cookies

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to the site owners.

  • First-Party Cookies – Set directly by aisimple.co. These cookies are under our control and are used to operate and improve our site.
  • Third-Party Cookies – Set by external services we use (such as analytics providers, advertising networks, or embedded content providers). These cookies are governed by the respective third party's privacy policy.

Cookies can also be classified by how long they persist:

  • Session Cookies – Temporary cookies that are deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent Cookies – Remain on your device for a set period or until you manually delete them.
03. Why We Use Cookies

Why Do We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Functionality – To enable core site features such as page navigation, secure access to member areas, and form submissions. The site cannot function properly without these cookies.
  • Analytics & Performance – To understand how visitors use our site, which pages are most popular, and how users move through the site. This helps us improve site performance and user experience.
  • Preferences & Personalization – To remember your settings and choices (such as language, region, or display preferences) so we can provide a more personalized experience on return visits.
  • Advertising & Targeting – To deliver relevant advertisements, measure ad effectiveness, and limit the number of times you see a particular ad.
04. Types of Cookies We Use

Types of Cookies We Use

The following categories describe the cookies that may be placed on your device when you visit our site:

  • Essential Cookies – Required for the website to function. These include cookies for authentication, session management, security, and load balancing. You cannot opt out of these cookies without affecting site functionality.
  • Performance / Analytics Cookies – Collect anonymized data about how visitors use the site, including pages visited, time spent on pages, and error messages encountered. We use this data to improve site performance. Examples include Google Analytics and similar tools.
  • Functional Cookies – Remember choices you make (such as login details, language preferences, or region) and provide enhanced, personalized features. These cookies may also be used to remember changes you have made to text size, fonts, and other customizable elements.
  • Targeting / Advertising Cookies – Used to deliver ads more relevant to you and your interests. They are also used to limit the number of times you see an ad, help measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns, and track whether you have visited an advertiser's site. These cookies are typically placed by advertising networks with the website operator's permission. Examples include Meta Pixel, Google Ads, and similar platforms.
05. How to Control Cookies

How to Control Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies (other than essential cookies). You can manage your cookie preferences in the following ways:

  • Browser Settings – Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to block or delete cookies, or to alert you when a cookie is being placed. Note that blocking all cookies may impair the functionality of our site.
  • Opt-Out Tools – Some third-party services offer their own opt-out mechanisms. For example, you can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on.

For instructions on managing cookies in your specific browser:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

If you use a different browser, please refer to that browser's help documentation for instructions on managing cookies.

06. Other Tracking Technologies

Other Tracking Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use other similar tracking technologies on our site:

  • Web Beacons (Tracking Pixels) – Small, transparent image files embedded in web pages or emails that allow us to track whether a page has been viewed or an email has been opened. Web beacons are often used in conjunction with cookies to measure the effectiveness of campaigns and site usage.
  • Pixels – Code snippets placed on our site that allow third-party platforms (such as Meta or Google) to track conversions, build audiences for ad targeting, and measure the performance of advertising campaigns.

These technologies work similarly to cookies and are subject to the same controls described in Section 5 above.

07. Flash Cookies / LSOs

Flash Cookies / Local Shared Objects (LSOs)

Flash cookies, also known as Local Shared Objects (LSOs), are data files similar to standard browser cookies but are stored by Adobe Flash Player. They may be used to store your preferences or display content based on what you view on our site.

Flash cookies are not managed through the same browser settings as standard cookies. To manage Flash cookies, visit the Adobe Flash Player Settings Manager. Please note that restricting Flash cookies may reduce or impede the functionality of some Flash-based applications on our site.

These third-party partners may use cookies and other tracking technologies to gather information about your activities on our site and other sites to provide you with targeted advertising based on your browsing activities and interests.

If you wish to opt out of interest-based advertising, you may visit the following:
